Here are three key ways to practice wicketkeeping stumpings effectively:

Master Footwork and Low Body Position: Stay low with your weight forward on the balls of your feet, moving smoothly with the bowler or spinner. Shuffle or take sharp, balanced steps to match the flight and turn of the ball so you are never caught flat-footed.

Keep Eyes Level and Hands Close: Watch the ball right onto the gloves, keeping your hands relaxed and close together. Absorb the impact by "giving" slightly with the ball rather than snatching at it, ensuring you cleanly gather it before breaking the stumps.

Break the Stumps with the Ball First: Practice sweeping the ball cleanly into the stumps in one fluid motion rather than rushing the throw. Aim for the middle stump using whichever hand holds the ball, ensuring the bails are dislodged safely while maintaining control.
